# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Residents of Quéven and the Lorient area (Morbihan, Bretagne), all ages, from young parents to seniors. They arrive via local search ("pharmacie Quéven", vaccination, ordonnance) often on mobile, and want to know: can this pharmacy help me, is it open, can I trust it. The site must feel like the counter itself: a familiar team that knows them.

## Product Purpose

Showcase Pharmacie Charnal (32 Place de Toulouse, Quéven), a family pharmacy of 40+ years. Success = visitors come to the pharmacy (CTAs always point to "venir en pharmacie", never "contactez-nous"), discover services (ordonnances, parapharmacie, vaccinations, produits naturels, matériel médical, livraison), use the health quizzes, and read the blog (SEO + AI visibility). Medical content is verified against the Vidal; precision is non-negotiable.

## Brand Personality

Familiale, experte, proche. Tradition and modernity together: the warmth of a neighborhood counter carried by a rigorous, professional presentation. Blog voice = Laure: conversational, frank, locally rooted. Three words: proche, rigoureuse, humaine.

## Anti-references

- The generic AI/SaaS landing template: identical icon-card grids, gradient text, pill eyebrows above every section, floating 3D blobs, ghost cards (1px border + huge blur shadow).
- The sterile hospital/clinic look: cold whites, corporate distance. The site is institutional in its order, never in its tone.
- Over-rounded, over-playful consumer web (32px radii, skewed card decks, gimmicks) that undermines medical trust.

## Design Principles

1. Confiance par l'ordre: institutional calm. Structured grids, hairline rules, generous whitespace, restrained color (teal, sage, cream). Trust comes from order, not decoration.
2. Proximité incarnée: real photos and video of the actual pharmacy and its people; copy that says "vous" and "chez nous". Warmth lives in imagery and words, not in gimmicks.
3. Une hiérarchie, pas une répétition: one featured element per composition (e.g. Délivrance d'ordonnances leads the services). Never six identical boxes.
4. Chaque section respire: err on more air. Space is the corporate signal the brand wants.
5. Précision partout: French typographic rules (ponctuation collée), verified medical claims, approved brand names only.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast (4.5:1 body, 3:1 large text). `prefers-reduced-motion` alternatives for every animation (video falls back to a still photo). Touch targets stay links with clear labels; no hover-only interactions that hide navigation.
